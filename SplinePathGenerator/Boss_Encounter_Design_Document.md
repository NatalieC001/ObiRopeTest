# 🐉 Boss Encounter Design Blueprint: The Asian Dragon Fight

This document outlines the strategic narrative and mechanical flow of the advanced boss encounters. It serves as the blueprint for how the `BossArenaManager`, `BossCreature`, and `SplineSwarmManager` interact to create a thrilling VR combat experience.

## 1. The Setup (The Scene)
*   **The Player:** Stands stationary on a Watchtower (VR constrained).
*   **The Environment:** The sky is filled with hand-drawn Dreamteck splines.
*   **The Crystal:** In the center of the sky is a massive crystal. Around it is a specific spline path.
*   **The Minions:** Smaller geometric swarms that protect the boss and crystal.

### The Boss (Segmented Asian Dragon)
Because the boss needs to slither fluidly through VR splines, it is designed as a **Segmented Creature**:
*   **Anatomy:** The dragon consists of a Head, a dynamically generated number of Body segments (some with legs/claws to animate), and a Tail.
*   **The Mechanic:** The player does not just shoot a single health bar. They must shoot away individual body segments.
*   **Dynamic Shrinking:** When a middle segment is destroyed, the dragon's overall power decreases. The remaining segments behind the destroyed piece smoothly slither forward along the spline to close the gap, physically shrinking the boss as the fight progresses.

---

## 2. The Battle Phases

### Phase 1: Arrogant Orchestration (Charging)
The Boss believes you are beneath its notice.
*   **Boss Action:** The Dragon loops continuously around the central Crystal Spline. It is absorbing energy to unleash a devastating attack. While charging, the Boss is heavily shielded or buffed by its active minions.
*   **Minion Action:** The swarm flies in beautiful geometric patterns (generated by the Spline Tool) around the boss, occasionally peeling off using `SplineSwarmManager` to dive-bomb the player on the watchtower.
*   **Player Strategy:** The player must survive the minion dive-bombs and thin their numbers. Shooting the boss now is ineffective due to the minion buffs.

### Phase 2: Panic & Engagement (The Attack)
Triggered when the player destroys a significant portion of the minions (e.g., 70% dead). The Boss realizes it is losing its protective swarm and attacks early before reaching full power.
*   **Boss Action:** The Dragon leaves the Crystal Spline. It slithers rapidly through the air directly at the player, entering attack range.
*   **Mechanic:** The Boss has a "Stamina" or "Energy" bar. Sustaining this close-quarters attack drains its energy rapidly.
*   **Player Strategy:** The player must frantically dodge/block the massive dragon sweeping over the watchtower while firing arrows into its vulnerable underbelly.

### Phase 3: Exhaustion & Evasion (Vulnerability)
Triggered when the Boss runs out of Stamina from attacking (or takes massive burst damage mid-fight).
*   **Boss Action:** The Dragon breaks off the attack. It uses the `TacticalBossSplineManager` to desperately slither through an environmental escape route (wrapping around a pillar, diving through clouds) to avoid player fire.
*   **The Goal:** The Dragon rides the escape route until it reaches the very end. At the end, it leaps back to the Observation Spline.
*   **Player Strategy:** *This is the critical damage window.* The Dragon is exhausted and fleeing. The player must use specific **Elemental Arrows** to counter the Dragon's elemental type as it flees through the environment, wearing its massive health pool down.

### Phase 4: Recharging
Triggered when the Dragon finishes the escape route and returns to the Observation Spline.
*   **Boss Action:** The Dragon slithers along the Observation Spline out of reach. Its Stamina bar begins to fill back up.
*   **Critical Mechanic:** *The boss regains Stamina, but it NEVER regains Health.* This prevents the fight from stalling infinitely.
*   **The Loop:** Once Stamina hits 100%, the boss dives back in to re-engage the player (returning to Phase 2).

---

## 3. Code Objectives to Achieve This

To make this encounter work, our scripts must achieve the following:

1.  **`BossArenaManager`:** Must monitor minion health, and rather than waiting for 0, it must trigger Phase 2 when the minion count drops below a specific percentage (e.g., <= 30%).
2.  **`BossCreature`:** Needs an "Energy" system. Energy drains during Phase 2. When empty, it triggers Phase 3 (Evasion).
3.  **`TacticalBossSplineManager`:** Must handle the fluid hopping between the attack vectors, the evasion pillars, and the return trip to the crystal.
4.  **`SplineSwarmManager`:** Already handles the minions peeling off to attack the player smoothly.

By structuring the fight this way, the VR player on the watchtower experiences a dynamic pacing of wave-clear (Phase 1), intense survival/dodging (Phase 2), and precise tactical sniping (Phase 3).
